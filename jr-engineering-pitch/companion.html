How to record the pitch video (<3 min)

  1. Open wargame demo in full screen. Start screen recorder (Loom, OBS, or Win+G).
  2. Narrate setup (20 sec): "North levee L-14, river at 12.4 ft, three response units staged."
  3. Press Play Scenario — let it run OR scrub timeline to T+52 for breach moment.
  4. Hit Trigger Breach if you want drama; show inundation stats climbing.
  5. Click Export CSV — "This is the deliverable our clients get in Excel."
  6. End (15 sec): flash companion hub URL. "Tier 1 is $160/month. Links in the deck."

Deploy & share (5 minutes)

  1. Push jr-engineering-pitch/ folder to GitHub.
  2. Connect repo to Vercel or Netlify — set root to jr-engineering-pitch.
  3. Get URL like jr-pitch.vercel.app — send Daniel the deck link.
  4. Optional: custom domain pitch.jrengineering.com via Cloudflare DNS.
